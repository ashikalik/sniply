Sniply

Create and track short links.

What you can do

Generate short links, track clicks in real time, and keep your campaigns organized with tags and custom aliases.

Why teams use Sniply

Simple URLs for marketing, rich analytics for growth, and predictable redirects for long-term campaigns.

Security

Password-protected links, expiration windows, and throttled click limits help control access.

Analytics

Capture referral data, device info, and UTM attributes to understand every visit.

Built for scale

Sniply is designed for high traffic with fast redirects and efficient data storage.
